  HERDR CHEATSHEET          press ctrl+b, let go, then the key
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  MY TOOLS
    f        explore project in Fresh        (quit: ctrl+q)
    d        diff uncommitted changes        (quit: q)
    alt+d    diff this branch vs main        (quit: q)

  PANES  (a pane = one terminal)
    v        split right
    minus    split down
    h j k l  move left / down / up / right
    z        zoom pane to full screen (again to restore)
    x        close pane

  TABS
    c        new tab
    n / p    next / previous tab
    1..9     jump to tab

  WORKSPACES  (one per project)
    w        pick a workspace
    shift+n  new workspace

  GENERAL
    q        detach (everything keeps running; `herdr` to return)
    ?        every keybinding
    i        this cheatsheet

  The mouse works too: click to focus, right-click for menus.

  Press q to close.
